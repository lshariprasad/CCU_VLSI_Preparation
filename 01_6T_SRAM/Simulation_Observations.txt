
PROJECT: 6T SRAM Cell Design and Simulation

Software: LTspice
Circuit: 6-Transistor SRAM Cell
Simulation: Transient Analysis
Supply Voltage: 1.8 V
Simulation Duration: 16 ns

OBSERVATIONS:

1. WRITE OPERATION:
The wordline is enabled.
BL is driven LOW and BLB is driven HIGH.
The SRAM storage nodes settle to complementary logic states.

2. HOLD OPERATION:
The wordline is disabled.
The storage nodes retain their previous logic states.

3. PRECHARGE OPERATION:
Both bitlines are charged HIGH before the read operation.

4. READ OPERATION:
The wordline is enabled again.
BL discharges while BLB remains HIGH.
This demonstrates the expected read behaviour for the stored data.

SIMULATION MODEL:
Generic Level-1 NMOS and PMOS models.

LIMITATION:
This is an educational simulation and does not use an actual
16-nm FinFET technology model.
