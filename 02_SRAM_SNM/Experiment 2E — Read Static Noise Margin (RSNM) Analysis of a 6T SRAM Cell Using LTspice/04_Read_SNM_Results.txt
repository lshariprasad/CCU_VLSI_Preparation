# EXPERIMENT 2E

## TITLE
Read-Mode Stability Study of a 6T SRAM Cell Using LTspice

## SOFTWARE
LTspice 26.0.1

## SUPPLY AND DEVICE MODEL
- Supply voltage: 1.8 V
- Generic Level-1 NMOS and PMOS models
- NMOS: W = 2 µm, L = 0.18 µm
- PMOS: W = 1 µm, L = 0.18 µm

## CURRENT FILE STATUS
A read-mode netlist, simulation log, raw data file, plot and this result template are present in the repository.

## ANALYSIS SETUP IN THE NETLIST
- Word line is set HIGH.
- Both bitlines are precharged HIGH.
- A DC source sweeps node Q from 0 V to 1.8 V in 1 mV steps.
- The circuit includes the two cross-coupled inverter pair and two access transistors.

## RESULT STATUS
**In progress - no verified numerical RSNM value is recorded.** The calculated-result field is intentionally left blank. The extraction method and exported butterfly data must be checked before an RSNM value is reported.

## LIMITATIONS
- The models are generic educational Level-1 models, not foundry-calibrated devices.
- This is not a 16-nm FinFET simulation.
- The presence of a netlist, log or plot alone does not establish a valid RSNM result.
- Do not claim a numerical RSNM or WSNM until the complete methodology and extracted result have been independently reviewed.
