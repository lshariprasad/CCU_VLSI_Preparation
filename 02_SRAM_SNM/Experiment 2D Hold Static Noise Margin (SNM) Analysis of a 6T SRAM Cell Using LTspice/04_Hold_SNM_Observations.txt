EXPERIMENT 2D

TITLE:
6T SRAM Hold-Mode Simulation and Preliminary Mirrored-Inverter VTC Estimate

SOFTWARE:
LTspice 26.0.1

SUPPLY VOLTAGE:
1.8 V

MOSFET MODEL:
Generic educational Level-1 NMOS and PMOS models (not a foundry-calibrated process model)

TRANSISTOR DIMENSIONS:
NMOS: W = 2 um, L = 0.18 um
PMOS: W = 1 um, L = 0.18 um

OPERATING MODE:
Hold mode for the complete 6T transient netlist

NETLIST SCOPE:
04_Hold_SNM_VTC.cir is a decoupled inverter VTC extraction setup; it is not the complete cross-coupled 6T cell.
05_6T_SRAM_Hold_Verification.cir contains the complete two-inverter, two-access-transistor 6T cell and a 20 ns transient-analysis setup.

VTC SWEEP:
0 V to 1.8 V in 1 mV steps (1801 points)

METHOD:
The second inverter characteristic was approximated by exchanging the X and Y coordinates of the extracted single-inverter VTC. This assumes identical inverter transfer characteristics.

RECORDED VALUE:
0.6442 V - preliminary mirrored-inverter VTC estimate only.

IMPORTANT RESULT LIMITATION:
The 0.6442 V value is NOT a validated cell-level 6T SRAM Hold Static Noise Margin (HSNM). A full-cell hold butterfly curve and a documented largest-square extraction are still required.

PROCESS LIMITATION:
These generic Level-1 models do not represent an actual 16-nm FinFET process and are not calibrated to a fabrication PDK.

VALIDATION NOTE:
The LTspice log for the complete 6T transient netlist records successful operating-point convergence. Inspect the transient waveform and rerun the simulation locally before reporting exact node voltages.
