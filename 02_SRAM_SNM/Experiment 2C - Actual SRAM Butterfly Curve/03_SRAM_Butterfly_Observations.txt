
EXPERIMENT 2C

TITLE:
Transistor-Level CMOS Inverter Butterfly Curve
for SRAM Hold-SNM Preparation

SOFTWARE: LTspice

SUPPLY VOLTAGE: 1.8 V

TRANSISTORS:
NMOS - Generic Level-1
PMOS - Generic Level-1

ANALYSIS: DC Sweep
INPUT RANGE: 0 V to 1.8 V
STEP SIZE: 1 mV

OBJECTIVE:
To obtain the transistor-level CMOS inverter
transfer characteristic and its mirrored curve.

OBSERVATIONS:

1. The inverter output decreases as input increases.
2. The mirrored characteristic increases as input increases.
3. Both characteristics intersect near the
   inverter switching point.
4. The two curves form a butterfly-shaped plot.

RESULT:
The transistor-level inverter characteristic and
its mirrored characteristic were simulated in LTspice.

LIMITATION:
Generic Level-1 MOSFET models were used.
This is not an actual 16-nm FinFET simulation
or a direct measurement of complete 6T SRAM SNM.
