
EXPERIMENT: CMOS INVERTER VTC

Software: LTspice
VDD: 1.8 V
NMOS: Generic Level-1
PMOS: Generic Level-1
Analysis: DC Sweep
Input sweep: 0 V to 1.8 V
Step: 1 mV

OBSERVATIONS:

1. At low input voltage, output voltage is HIGH.
2. At high input voltage, output voltage is LOW.
3. A sharp switching transition occurs around 0.75 V.

RESULT:
The CMOS inverter voltage transfer characteristic
was successfully obtained using LTspice.

LIMITATION:
Generic MOSFET models were used, not 16-nm FinFET models.
